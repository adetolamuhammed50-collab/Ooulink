Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

StudTask (“StudTask”, “we”, “us”) provides a student task marketplace. This policy explains the information we collect, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, username, university or campus information, profile information, task information, messages, reviews, verification information, payment and withdrawal records, and information you submit when reporting or disputing activity.

2. Waitlist information

If you join the launch waitlist, we collect the name, email address, phone number, and optional campus information you submit. We use this information to communicate launch updates and related StudTask announcements.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide and secure StudTask, authenticate accounts, operate tasks and payments, process verification and moderation, send relevant notifications, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, and communicate important service updates.

4. Payments

Payment-provider processing is handled through the providers integrated with StudTask. StudTask does not ask you to enter payment-provider secret keys into the browser. Payment records may be stored so transactions can be reconciled and supported.

5. Verification

If you request student verification, you may be asked for a student ID image, selfie, matric number, and course. These materials are used for verification and platform safety and should not contain unnecessary information.

6. Sharing

We may share information with service providers needed to operate StudTask, such as authentication, hosting, database, storage, communication, and payment providers. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users, investigate abuse, or enforce our rules.

7. Security

We use access controls and technical measures intended to protect account and transaction data. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain transaction and safety records, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Account deletion requests may be handled through StudTask's account controls and administrative process.

9. Your choices

You can update certain profile information through StudTask. You may also request help with account or communication preferences through StudTask support when available.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as StudTask changes. The date above indicates the latest revision.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the support/contact method provided by StudTask in the app or launch communications.
