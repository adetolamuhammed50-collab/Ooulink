How your balance works

Deposited money becomes available for posting tasks. When a task is funded, its amount is reserved. After the runner completes the task and the poster verifies it, the task payment can be released to the runner's withdrawable balance.

Real deposits and withdrawals are not enabled yet. The wallet ledger is live, but connecting a payment/payout provider requires the provider's approved account and webhook configuration.
